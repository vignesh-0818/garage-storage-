GarageOrg Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 15, 2026

At GarageOrg, we value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, handle, and protect your information when you browse our website, request a space planning consultation, or engage our garage organization and storage installation services.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that is reasonably necessary to provide tailored storage consultations, structural spatial planning, custom estimates, and installation services:

  • Contact Details: Your full name, email address, telephone number, and residential street address for on-site garage inspections.
  • Garage Specifications: Garage layout measurements, ceiling heights, wall construction types (stud, drywall, masonry), vehicle dimensions, and photos provided during consultation inquiries.
  • Project Preferences: Selected cabinetry configurations, shelving weight tier preferences, overhead rack clearance requirements, and scheduling requests.
  • Website Usage Analytics: Anonymized browser type, device specifications, language settings, and page navigation statistics used strictly to enhance our website responsiveness.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information gathered through our forms and communications exclusively for legitimate business purposes:

  • Generating tailored 3D space layouts, weight distribution calculations, and cost estimates.
  • Scheduling in-home site assessments, delivery of modular steel units, and on-site installations.
  • Communicating project milestones, warranty registrations, and maintenance advice.
  • Sending requested newsletters or seasonal storage organization guides (you may unsubscribe at any time).

3. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share project data with trusted service partners directly involved in delivering your project, such as:

  • Certified local installation technicians and structural mounting specialists.
  • Direct freight logistics carriers delivering heavy steel cabinetry or overhead racks to your home.
  • Secure cloud infrastructure providers hosting our CAD files and customer records under strict confidentiality agreements.

4. Data Security & Storage Integrity

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security safeguards to protect your personal contact information and property photos from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Digital communications transmitted through our consultation portal are encrypted via Transport Layer Security (TLS/SSL).

5. Cookies & Local Storage Preferences

Our website utilizes lightweight browser local storage to remember your visual display preferences—specifically your Dark Mode / Light Mode preference and Left-to-Right (LTR) / Right-to-Left (RTL) text direction choices. These client-side cookies do not track your activity across unrelated websites.

6. Your Privacy Rights & Access

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal contact details and garage CAD data we hold in your file.
  • Request correction or updating of any outdated or incomplete contact information.
  • Request deletion of your consultation history following completion of your project.

7. Contact Our Privacy Team

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your privacy rights, please reach out to our team:
