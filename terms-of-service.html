GarageOrg Terms of Service

Last Updated: January 15, 2026

These Terms of Service govern your use of the GarageOrg website and the provision of garage space planning, shelving, cabinetry, slatwall, overhead rack installation, and decluttering services. By requesting a consultation, placing an order, or scheduling an on-site installation, you agree to these terms.

1. Consultations, Proposals & Estimates

All preliminary estimates provided via phone, email, or online forms are non-binding ballpark assessments based on client-provided approximations. A final, binding project proposal is issued only after on-site laser measurements, stud inspection, and physical assessment of ceiling joists and wall substrates.

  • Proposals remain valid for thirty (30) calendar days from the date of issuance.
  • Any modifications to cabinetry layout, accessory additions, or floor finishes requested after contract signing will be documented in a written Change Order.

2. Site Access & Pre-Installation Readiness

To ensure safe, efficient, and timely installation, the client agrees to:

  • Provide clear, unobstructed access to the garage, driveway, and standard electrical outlets on the scheduled installation date.
  • Unless full decluttering service is explicitly included in the contract, ensure vehicles and personal belongings are removed from work areas prior to technician arrival.
  • Notify GarageOrg of any known hidden in-wall plumbing, embedded conduits, or structural anomalies prior to fastening uprights or ceiling anchors.

3. Structural Fastening & Load Capacity Limits

GarageOrg storage components are engineered and rated according to strict static load tolerances (e.g., up to 800 lbs per custom shelf span and up to 600 lbs per ceiling overhead platform when mounted into certified structural wood or steel joists).

Clients must not exceed published maximum load capacities or hang swinging dynamic loads from ceiling platforms. GarageOrg is not liable for structural failures resulting from customer overloading, unauthorized relocation of anchored brackets, or modification of safety pins.

4. Payment Terms & Schedule

Standard payment terms for custom installation projects:

  • Deposit: A 50% deposit is required upon proposal approval to secure material fabrication and reserve your installation date.
  • Final Balance: The remaining 50% balance is due upon successful project completion and walkthrough inspection.

5. Limited Workmanship Warranty

GarageOrg warrants all custom steel cabinetry, wall tracks, and overhead rack hardware installations against defects in workmanship and structural fastening for a period of ten (10) years from the date of completion. Powder-coat finishes and accessories are covered under manufacturer warranties against peeling or corrosion under standard residential conditions.

Warranty excludes normal cosmetic wear and tear, chemical spills, moisture damage from unresolved foundation leaks, or alterations made by third parties.

6. Rescheduling & Cancellations

We understand that unexpected schedule conflicts arise. Clients may reschedule installation appointments without penalty up to forty-eight (48) hours prior to the scheduled start time. Cancellations of custom-fabricated orders after materials have entered production may be subject to a restocking fee for non-standard items.
