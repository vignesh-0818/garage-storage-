Custom Garage Shelving Systems

Our custom garage shelving systems are engineered to turn raw wall capacity into high-efficiency storage. Built using industrial powder-coated steel uprights and solid steel or heavy-duty timber deck inlays, each shelf can carry up to 800 lbs with zero deflection.

Key Solution Features & Engineering Specs

  • Adjustable shelf brackets with 1.5-inch vertical increments
  • Continuous wall-track mounting distributes weight across studs
  • Corrosion-resistant matte black powder coat finish
  • Custom sizing tailored around outlets, doors, and vehicle clearances

Installation Process

From Measurement to Finished System

A systematic workflow that guarantees clean, secure installations.

1
Laser Stud Mapping

Electronic scanning to locate internal studs and structural headers.

2
Horizontal Rail Fastening

Anchoring the primary load-bearing horizontal steel rail into every stud.

3
Vertical Track Hang

Locking slotted uprights into position and fastening bottom stabilizer points.

4
Shelf Decking & QA

Installing steel decks, safety lock clips, and verifying complete level.
